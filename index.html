⚠ Read before use

This is a personal self-tracking aid, not a medical device.

  • Set your phone volume once to a comfortable maximum, then don't touch it again. The app does all the volume changes — adjusting the phone too will break tracking.
  • Volume is shown as 0–100% of the app's output at your fixed phone volume — a consistent scale for tracking progress, not a real-world loudness measurement.
  • Follow the noise-exposure program and volume limits given by your audiologist. Do not exceed what they prescribed.
  • If pain, ringing, or hearing changes worsen, stop and contact your clinician.

How levels work

Volume is shown as 0–100%. It's scaled by perceived loudness, so a slow ramp feels gradual the whole way up. 100% is the app's full output at the phone volume you've set.

Set the phone volume once and keep it fixed. With the same headphones and a fixed phone volume, % is consistent session to session — so it's a reliable way to track progress over time.

Data

Everything is stored only in this browser. Back up regularly.

On iPhone, don't try to open the saved file — iOS has no app for .json, that's normal. To move data to another device, use Copy backup here and Paste & restore there. Or Export / Share → Save to Files, then later Import file and pick it.

Phone tips

  • On iPhone, turn the silent/ring switch off silent or audio may not play.
  • The app keeps the screen awake during a session where supported; don't lock the phone manually mid-session or audio may stop.
  • Add to Home Screen for a full-screen, app-like launch.

Medical disclaimer

This is a personal self-tracking aid, not a medical device. The 0–100% scale is relative to the app's output at your phone volume, not a real-world loudness measurement. Follow your audiologist's prescribed program and volume limits. If symptoms worsen, stop and consult your clinician.
